# Asset provenance

This repository includes original and generated visual assets used by the simulation.

- `public/assets/ship.glb` and `public/assets/planet.glb` are AI-assisted 3D assets developed through multi-view Hunyuan3D workflows and subsequently integrated into the scene.
- The interface's space and surface treatments are original generated concept artwork.
- `public/assets/stars.json` is derived from the public Bright Star Catalogue (BSC5).
- `public/assets/sky-targets.json` uses target coordinates retrieved from NASA Exoplanet Archive TAP `pscomppars` data on 2026-09-17.

If you identify an attribution or licensing issue, please open an issue or contact `dev@smalltownrobotics.com`.
