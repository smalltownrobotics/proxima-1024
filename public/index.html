A world between stars.

You sleep. Generations live. Jev watches the ship and evaluates every living crew member’s response to your decisions. Astra interprets your orders and talks through the consequences.

This is an experimental fictional simulation, not a forecast of spaceflight or human behavior. The annual engine owns resources, births, deaths and failures; bounded game rules own interventions. No outcome is scripted.

Jev responses are live API results, not canned animation. Dot colors show each person’s most likely response; the bars and social effects use the full probability distribution. Only fictional game state goes to the model APIs.

The weathered hulls reuse our local Hunyuan models with new surface texturing and concept habitat assemblies. Planet surfaces, atmospheres and the commissioning sky are fictional artwork, not observations or engineering validation.

The navigation chart excludes that illustrative sky. It uses NASA Exoplanet Archive directions, catalog route distances and BSC5 stars. Ship and star symbols are enlarged for legibility; this is not a navigation solution.
